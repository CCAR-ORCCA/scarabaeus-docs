ThermalRadiation#

class ThermalRadiation(origin: CelestialBody, spacecraft: Spacecraft, base_frame: Frame, thermal_model, t0: float = None, tf: float = None)#

Bases: DynamicModel

Recoil acceleration from the thermal radiation the spacecraft emits.

Each surface \(i\) of area \(A_i\), emissivity \(\varepsilon_i\) and outward normal \(\hat{\mathbf{n}}_i\) radiates as a Lambertian emitter at temperature \(T_i\). The recoil of that emission is

\[\mathbf{a}_i = -\frac{2}{3}\, \frac{\sigma A_i \varepsilon_i}{m\,c}\,T_i^4\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}_i\]

where \(\sigma\) is the Stefan-Boltzmann constant, \(m\) the spacecraft mass and \(c\) the speed of light. The factor 2/3 is the momentum a Lambertian surface carries away along its normal. The total acceleration is

\[\mathbf{a}_\text{therm} = \eta_\text{therm} \sum_i \mathbf{a}_i\]

with \(\eta_\text{therm}\) an estimable scale factor (default 1), read from the state under ("eta_thermal", spice_id).

Temperatures. A surface temperature is read from a table of temperature against off-Sun angle \(\theta\), given at a few Sun-spacecraft distances \(d\):

\[T_i = T_{\text{table}(i)}\!\left(\theta_i,\, d\right),\qquad \cos\theta_i = \hat{\boldsymbol{\rho}}_i\cdot\hat{\mathbf{u}}_\odot\]

where \(\hat{\mathbf{u}}_\odot\) is the spacecraft-to-Sun unit vector and \(\hat{\boldsymbol{\rho}}_i\) the table’s reference direction: the table’s angle_reference when it has one (for example the spacecraft axis the thermal analysis measured the off-Sun angle from), otherwise the surface’s own normal. Each distance row is a cubic spline in \(\theta\), with zero slope imposed at 0 and 180 degrees when the table reaches them, since a temperature is symmetric about those angles. Rows are joined by linear interpolation in \(d\). Outside the tabulated angles and distances the temperature is held at the nearest edge.

A surface can carry a second, shadow_temperature table, used when \(\hat{\mathbf{n}}_i\cdot\hat{\mathbf{u}}_\odot \le 0\). This is how a high-gain antenna built from many plates picks an illuminated or a shadowed temperature for each plate.

Attitude. Normals and angle references are fixed in the spacecraft frame and reach the inertial frame through the spacecraft-fixed to inertial rotation \(C\) of DynamicalUtils, for the "CK", "inertialFixedAttitude" and "nadirPointingSun" values of Spacecraft.attitudeMode. In the last one the spacecraft \(-X\) axis points at the Sun and the Y axis follows the heliocentric velocity, so \(C\) depends on the state, the partials carry the rotation terms and the model reports depends_on_velocity.

Thermal model. thermal_model is a dict, or the path of a JSON file holding one, with two entries:

"temperature_tables"

{name: table}, each table holding

  • "angles": ArrayWUnits of off-Sun angles, strictly increasing within [0, 180] degrees, at least two;

  • "distances": ArrayWUnits of Sun distances, strictly increasing, at least one;

  • "temperatures": array of shape (n_distances, n_angles) (a single distance may be given as a 1-D array);

  • "temperature_units": "K" or "degC";

  • "angle_reference" (optional): spacecraft-fixed direction the off-Sun angle is measured from.

"surfaces"

list of dicts, each holding

  • "name": label;

  • "area": ArrayWUnits area;

  • "emissivity": value in [0, 1];

  • "normal": outward normal in the spacecraft frame;

  • "temperature": name of its temperature table;

  • "shadow_temperature" (optional): name of the table used when the surface faces away from the Sun.

Quantities with units may also be given in their serialized form ({"values", "units"}, see awu_to_dict()), which is what model_to_dict() writes.

Notes

The position partial carries the temperature change with the Sun distance and the off-Sun angle, and, for the Sun-pointed attitude, the rotation of the surface normals. The scale-factor partial is the acceleration with \(\eta_\text{therm} = 1\).

Switching a surface between its illuminated and shadowed tables is a step in the acceleration; the partials take the table in use.

References

exception InvalidSurfaceError#

Bases: Exception

A surface is malformed.

A surface needs a name, a positive area quantity, an emissivity in [0, 1], a nonzero spacecraft-fixed normal and a temperature table.

exception InvalidTemperatureTableError#

Bases: Exception

A temperature table is malformed.

Angles must be an angle quantity strictly increasing within [0, 180] degrees, distances a length quantity strictly increasing and positive, temperatures of shape (n_distances, n_angles) and above absolute zero, and the temperature units "K" or "degC".

exception InvalidThermalModelError#

Bases: Exception

thermal_model must be a dict, or the path of a JSON file holding one, with "temperature_tables" and "surfaces" entries.

Bases: Exception
