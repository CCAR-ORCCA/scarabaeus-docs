MCMC#

class MCMC(filter_obj: FilterOD | SolutionOD, verbose: bool = True, *, walkers_per_dimension: int = 4, min_walkers: int = 32, walker_spread: float = 1.0, max_initial_redraws: int = 100, stretch_scale: float = 2.0, de_weights: tuple = (0.7, 0.3), burn_in_factor: float = 2.0, thin_factor: float = 0.5, tau_multiple: float = 50, min_acceptance: float = 0.2)#

Bases: FilterOD

Markov chain Monte Carlo sampling of a converged orbit determination.

A filter (LSB, SRIFB, LKF, SRIF, …) is first fitted to convergence. Its last pass fixes the reference trajectory, the prefit residuals \(y_k\), the measurement partials \(H_k\) and the STMs \(\Phi(t_k, t_0)\). About that reference the measurements are linear in the deviation \(x\) at the reference epoch,

\[y_k = A_k x + \epsilon_k, \qquad A_k = H_k \Phi(t_k, t_0), \qquad \epsilon_k \sim \mathcal{N}(0, R_k),\]

and the posterior of \(x\) is sampled with the affine-invariant ensemble sampler of emcee:

\[\log p(x \mid y) = -\frac{1}{2} \sum_k (y_k - A_k x)^T R_k^{-1} (y_k - A_k x) + \log p(x) + \mathrm{const}.\]

No trajectory is propagated and no measurement is recomputed while sampling: every step costs two small matrix products. The data enter through the normal matrix and vector

\[D = \sum_k A_k^T R_k^{-1} A_k, \qquad d = \sum_k A_k^T R_k^{-1} y_k.\]

Whitening

The walkers move in \(\theta\), with \(x = \hat{x} + L\theta\), where \(\hat{x} = M^{-1} b\) and \(L L^T = M^{-1}\) are the batch least-squares solution and covariance (\(M = \bar{P}_0^{-1} + D\), \(b = \bar{P}_0^{-1} \bar{x}_0 + d\)). With the filter’s Gaussian a-priori the log-posterior is then \(-\tfrac{1}{2}\theta^T\theta\), whatever the conditioning of \(M\). A user prior \(\log p\) replaces the Gaussian a-priori, and the log-posterior becomes

\[-\frac{1}{2} \theta^T L^T D L\, \theta + \theta^T L^T (d - D \hat{x}) + \log p(\hat{x} + L\theta - \bar{x}_0),\]

up to a constant. The prior is evaluated on the deviation from the a-priori state, so a Gaussian \(\log p(\delta) = -\tfrac{1}{2}\delta^T \bar{P}_0^{-1}\delta\) reproduces the default.

Sequences

For a MissionSequence every leg is mapped to one global vector \(z\): the first leg’s state at its epoch, followed by each block a later leg adds at its node, with its a-priori sigma. Leg \(j\) is \(x_j = T_j z\), with \(T_j = Q_j \Phi_{j-1}(t_{node}) T_{j-1}\) plus an identity on the blocks the node adds, so a measurement of leg \(j\) has \(A_k = H_k \Phi_j(t_k) T_j\). This is the batch of LSB, with every leg mapped back through the nodes, as long as no considered state is dropped at a node: a dropped one stays considered in \(z\), whereas the filter folds its spread into the next leg’s a priori and lets that leg’s data reduce it (ConsiderDroppedWarning).

Consider parameters

Considered states are not sampled. The walkers sample the estimated states with the considered ones at their reference, and the consider spread is added linearly as in the Schmidt batch: each particle draws \(c \sim \mathcal{N}(0, P_{cc})\) and is shifted by \(S_{xc} c\), with \(S_{xc} = -M_{xx}^{-1} M_{xc}\).

Solution

sample() returns a SolutionOD whose deviation and covariance histories are the chain mean and covariance (plus the consider part) mapped to every epoch with the STMs, and whose filter carries particles (the posterior samples mapped the same way), so SolutionOD.uq_metrics is filled. The samples at the reference epoch are also stored as samples, ready for plot_corner().

Parameters:
filter_objFilterOD or SolutionOD

A filter fitted to convergence with FilterOD.fit(), or the solution it returned. It is copied, not modified.

verbosebool, optional

Print the initialization banner. Defaults to True.

walkers_per_dimensionint, optional

Walkers per estimated state when sample() is not given n_walkers. The stretch move needs more than twice the dimension; four times keeps the ensemble from collapsing onto a subspace. Defaults to 4.

min_walkersint, optional

Fewest walkers when sample() is not given n_walkers. Defaults to 32.

walker_spreadfloat, optional

Spread of the initial walkers, in units of the filter’s 1-sigma. The walkers are drawn from the converged filter’s Gaussian scaled by this factor, so with 1 the chain starts close to stationarity on the linearized problem and the burn-in is short. Defaults to 1.0.

max_initial_redrawsint, optional

Times an initial walker is redrawn when the log-posterior is not finite at its position (a log_prior that rules out part of the Gaussian) before sample() gives up. Defaults to 100.

stretch_scalefloat, optional

Scale parameter \(a\) of the affine-invariant stretch move of moves="stretch", the default of Goodman & Weare (2010) and of emcee. Defaults to 2.0.

de_weightstuple of float, optional

Relative weights of the differential-evolution and snooker moves of moves="de"; emcee normalizes them. Defaults to (0.7, 0.3).

burn_in_factorfloat, optional

Burn-in, in multiples of the largest integrated autocorrelation time of the chain, and at most half the chain. Defaults to 2.0.

thin_factorfloat, optional

Thinning, in multiples of the smallest integrated autocorrelation time of the chain, and at least one step. Defaults to 0.5.

tau_multiplefloat, optional

Chain length, in largest integrated autocorrelation times, below which the autocorrelation estimate (and with it burn-in and thinning) is not trusted, and ShortChainWarning is issued. The tol default of emcee. Defaults to 50.

min_acceptancefloat, optional

Mean acceptance fraction below which the ensemble mixes poorly and AcceptanceFractionWarning is issued. There is no upper bound: the stretch move scales its steps with the spread of the ensemble, so a high acceptance does not mean short steps. On the whitened, near-Gaussian posterior of a few states it is about 0.5 or more. Defaults to 0.2.

Notes

The sampled posterior is the linearized one: the dynamics and measurement models are not re-evaluated, so the chain reproduces the filter’s Gaussian unless a non-Gaussian prior is given. Its value is in the prior and in a sample-based uncertainty description consistent with the particle filters.

The source filter’s smoother, gain and innovation histories describe its own run, not the sampled posterior, and are not carried over.

The sampler settings, walkers_per_dimension to min_acceptance, are kept as attributes of the same name, so they can also be changed between calls to sample().

sample(n_steps: int = 5000, n_walkers: int | None = None, moves='stretch', log_prior: Callable[[ndarray], float] | None = None, n_particles: int = 1000, seed: int | None = None, meas_corr: list | None = None, progress: bool = False, verbose: bool = True) → SolutionOD#

Sample the linearized posterior and pack it as a solution.

Parameters:
n_stepsint, optional

Steps of each walker. Defaults to 5000.

n_walkersint, optional

Number of walkers, at least twice the number of estimated states. Defaults to walkers_per_dimension per estimated state, and at least min_walkers.

movesstr or emcee move(s), optional

"stretch" (affine-invariant stretch move, scale stretch_scale), "de" (differential evolution mixed with snooker moves, de_weights), or any moves argument of emcee.EnsembleSampler. Defaults to "stretch".

log_priorcallable, optional

Log-density of the prior, called on the deviation of the estimated states from their a-priori value (a 1-D array, in the order of the estimated entries of labels). It replaces the filter’s Gaussian a-priori; return -np.inf outside the support. Defaults to the Gaussian a-priori.

n_particlesint, optional

Posterior samples kept as particles. Drawn from the burnt-in, thinned chain, with replacement only if it is shorter. Defaults to 1000.

seedint, optional

Seed of the initial walkers, the sampler, the particle draw and the consider draws. Defaults to None (not reproducible).

meas_corrlist, optional

Measurement correlation factors, as in FilterOD.fit(). Defaults to None.

progressbool, optional

Show the emcee progress bar. Defaults to False.

verbosebool, optional

Print the chain diagnostics. Defaults to True.

Returns:
SolutionOD

The chain mean and covariance mapped to every epoch, the residuals about the mean, and SolutionOD.uq_metrics of the particles. It is not saved; call SolutionOD.save().

Raises:
SampleSizeError

If n_steps or n_particles is not positive.

NoEstimatedStatesError

If every state is considered.

WalkerCountError

If n_walkers is less than twice the number of estimated states.

UnknownMoveError

If moves is an unknown name.

PosteriorCovarianceError

If the batch covariance is not positive definite.

InitialWalkersError

If log_prior is not finite at the redrawn initial walkers.

ConsiderPartitionError

If a state changes between estimated and considered at a node.

AprioriSigmaSizeError

If a block added at a node has too few a-priori sigmas.

Warns:
ShortChainWarning

If the chain is short compared with its autocorrelation time.

AcceptanceFractionWarning

If the mean acceptance fraction is below min_acceptance.

ConsiderDroppedWarning

If a considered state is dropped at a sequence node.

Notes

Besides the solution this sets samples (global state at the reference epoch, as deviations from the converged reference), chain (every step of every walker, estimated states only, shape (n_steps, n_walkers, n_estimated)), log_prob, diagnostics, posterior_mean and posterior_covariance. particles has shape (n_particles, n, n_epochs), zero-padded on legs with fewer states.

Bases: ValueError

exception ConsiderDroppedWarning#

Bases: UserWarning

A considered state leaves the state vector at a sequence node. The chain keeps it considered over the later legs, whereas the sequential filters fold its spread into the next leg’s a priori, where that leg’s data reduce it, so the sampled posterior is not the filter’s. Keep the considered state in the later legs to match the filter, or silence it with warnings.filterwarnings("ignore", category=MCMC.ConsiderDroppedWarning).
