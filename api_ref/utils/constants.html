RLT_BODIES = (('399', 398600.435436096 mu), ('301', 4902.8000661638 mu), ('4', 42828.375214 mu), ('5', 126712764.8 mu), ('6', 37940585.2 mu))#

Default bodies of the relativistic light-time delay, as (spice_id, GM) pairs, besides the Sun.

Earth and the Moon are taken at their centres, as the station sits next to them. Mars, Jupiter and Saturn are taken at their system barycentres with the system GM, so that only the planetary ephemeris is needed, not the satellite kernels. For a Mars orbiter (DSS-34 tracking, 2020-21 and 2025) this moves the round-trip light time by at most 2.1 ps, 0.63 mm of round-trip path, mostly Jupiter’s GM difference, and the 60 s X-band Doppler by less than 1e-6 mHz. Close to Jupiter or Saturn the barycentre is a poor stand-in for the planet (an orbiter at 1.1 planet radii sees up to 3 cm and 0.25 mHz), so pass the planet centres there.
